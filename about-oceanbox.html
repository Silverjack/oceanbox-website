From a listing to a confirmed purchase

Match the city and state or province, container specification, quantity and timing first. Confirm the price, currency, relevant charges and pickup or gate-buy arrangement in the written offer. Before dispatching a truck, obtain the applicable release reference and depot instructions.

Published quantities can include expected arrivals. An ETA is a planning date; obtain confirmation that equipment is ready before arranging collection. Our pickup guide explains the sequence, and our condition guide covers the questions to ask about one-trip, cargo-worthy and as-is units.
