Oceanbox Equipment Guide

40ft High Cube Side-Opening Containers

Ask Oceanbox Logistic Limited about side-opening equipment, including 40HCOS 4-door configurations, for your location in the United States or Canada.

Specify the door arrangement you need

A side-opening configuration provides access along the container side. The exact number, position and clear opening of the doors matter when planning loading, storage or a conversion. Include “40HCOS 4-door” in your inquiry if that is the configuration you require, and ask for photographs or a drawing of the proposed equipment.

Compare specifications before ordering

Request the external and internal dimensions, clear door openings, floor condition and loading limits applicable to the offered container. If it will carry cargo, specify the shipment and any required inspection or certification. If it will be modified, provide your project requirements before accepting the equipment.

Side-opening, double-door and Duocon listings describe different arrangements. Do not substitute one based only on nominal container length; check the actual doors and dimensions.

Check availability by city and configuration

Use the published inventory to review location, type, specification and listing notes. Send the city and state or province, quantity, required door layout, condition preference and required date to Oceanbox. Availability and price depend on the written offer; this guide is not a reservation or a live stock promise.

Confirm condition and collection terms

Ask whether photographs show the actual offered units or a representative example, and confirm defects, door operation and any required acceptance criteria. Review the condition guide when comparing one-trip, cargo-worthy and as-is descriptions.

An expected arrival date does not authorize pickup. Confirm the applicable charges, release reference and depot instructions before sending a truck. Our pickup guide explains the sequence.

Request a specific offer from Oceanbox

Request a quote with your location, quantity and configuration. You can also contact rolly@oceanbox.cn. Review Oceanbox company information and public identity records as part of your supplier checks.
